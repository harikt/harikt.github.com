### 🧩 Maintainer of Aura for PHP

I am one of the maintainers of [**Aura**](https://auraphp.com/), the PHP framework and library collection created by [**Paul M Jones**](https://paul-m-jones.com/)—a prominent figure in the PHP community, author of *Modernizing Legacy Applications in PHP*, and well known for his work on PHP framework benchmarking and performance.

Aura's packages are fully decoupled and dependency-free, so each one can be used standalone or composed into a full framework. I have contributed code, documentation and releases across the project for over a decade, and much of the [writing on this blog](/tags/aura/) comes out of that work.
