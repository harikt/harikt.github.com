I am **Hari KT**, a **freelancer** and full stack developer based in Kerala, India—God's own country. I work remotely with clients around the world, from solo founders validating a first idea to established teams who need an extra pair of experienced hands.

I have been freelancing since 2013, writing PHP since 2006, and publishing what I learn on this site since 2012.

## What I do

I take products across the whole stack rather than a single slice of it—database design, backend APIs, frontend interfaces, and deployment. In practice that means a client can hand over a problem instead of a task list.

**Backend.** PHP is my core expertise, alongside **Golang**, **Python** and **NodeJS**. I have worked with most of the major PHP frameworks and, more often, with decoupled standalone components composed into exactly what a project needs. On the Python side I build APIs with **FastAPI** and handle web scraping and data extraction with **Scrapy**.

**Frontend and mobile.** **ReactJS** on the web, **React Native** for Android and iOS, and **Tauri** for cross-platform desktop applications on Windows, macOS and Linux. Because these share a common React foundation, I can take one product to web, desktop and mobile without handing it to a different team for each platform.

**AI-assisted development.** I use AI tooling for rapid prototyping and to move from idea to working software faster.

Much of my work involves composing decoupled, framework-agnostic components—an approach shaped by years on Aura—so the tool fits the problem instead of the other way round. You will find hands-on posts about many of these under [frameworks](/tags/frameworks/) and [architecture](/tags/architecture/).

## Open source

### Maintainer of Aura for PHP

I am one of the maintainers of [**Aura for PHP**](https://auraphp.com/), the framework and library collection created by [**Paul M Jones**](https://paul-m-jones.com/)—a prominent figure in the PHP community, author of *Modernizing Legacy Applications in PHP*, and well known for his work on PHP framework benchmarking and performance.

Aura's packages are fully decoupled and dependency-free, so each one works standalone or composes into a complete framework. I have contributed code, documentation and releases across the project for over a decade, and a good deal of the [writing on this blog](/tags/aura/) grew out of that work.

### Composer support for ProcessWire

I built a [Composer installer for ProcessWire modules](https://github.com/harikt/pwmoduleinstaller), bringing proper dependency management to a CMS that had been installing third-party modules by hand. The work was picked up and referenced by the ProcessWire team in their own blog post, [Composer, Google Calendars and ProcessWire](https://processwire.com/blog/posts/composer-google-calendars-and-processwire/).

I wrote up the approach here in [Composer Support for ProcessWire Modules](/blog/2013/11/16/composer-support-for-processwire-modules/) and a [follow-up covering repositories without a `composer.json`](/blog/2013/11/19/composer-support-for-processwire-part-2/)—see everything under [ProcessWire](/tags/processwire/).
